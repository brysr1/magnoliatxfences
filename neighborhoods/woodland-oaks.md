Woodland Oaks is one of the many HOA-governed neighborhoods we serve in the Magnolia area, and homeowners here want a fence that looks good, lasts, and fits community standards. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds and repairs fences for homeowners in Woodland Oaks with a focus on clean workmanship and HOA-compliant design.

## Starting With Your Community's Guidelines

Neighborhood design guidelines typically spell out fence height, approved materials, stain colors, and setbacks from the street. Rather than leaving you to figure that out on your own, we review your goals and help you choose a fence style that fits your community's requirements. We'll provide the details you need for your approval request, then build the fence to match what was approved.

Fence styles that work well for homes in Woodland Oaks include:

- **Cedar privacy fences** with an optional top cap and trim for a finished look
- **Board-on-board privacy fencing** that stays gap-free as the wood weathers
- **Iron and aluminum fencing** for pool enclosures and front or side yards where you want an open view
- **Custom gates** for walkways, side yards, and equipment access

## Fences That Match the Neighborhood

Consistency is a big part of what makes a neighborhood look well kept. When your fence connects to a neighbor's or replaces part of an existing builder fence, we match height, post spacing, and finish so the new work blends in. If your community specifies a stain color, we can finish your fence to match.

## Durable Construction for Southeast Texas

Our clay soils swell and shrink with the weather, and that movement is hard on fence posts. We set posts properly, use hardware that resists rust, and brace gates so they keep closing cleanly. The goal is a fence that still looks right years from now.

## Repairs and Partial Replacements

Not every problem requires a whole new fence. For homeowners in Woodland Oaks, we repair leaning sections, replace damaged pickets and posts, and rehang gates. When replacement is the better value, we'll quote it in the same approved style.
