Homeowners in Westwood want fences that give their families privacy and their pets a safe yard, without running into trouble with neighborhood rules. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs and repairs HOA-compliant fences in Westwood and throughout the Magnolia area, with every project planned around your community's guidelines and the look of the homes around you.

## Planning a Fence That Gets Approved

Most HOA-governed neighborhoods in Magnolia, including Westwood, have guidelines covering fence height, material, color, and where a fence can be placed on the lot. We start by learning what you need the fence to do, then recommend a style that fits within your community's standards. You'll get a clear description of the materials, height, and layout to include with your approval request, so there's no guesswork.

Fence options we commonly install for homeowners in Westwood include:

- **Wood privacy fences** in cedar or pine, in side-by-side or board-on-board styles
- **Stained and sealed finishes** to protect the wood and match approved colors
- **Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing** for pools and open-view areas
- **Walk gates and double gates** built with proper bracing so they don't sag

## Matching Your Neighborhood's Look

A new fence should look like it belongs. When we replace a section, extend a fence line, or connect to a neighbor's fence, we match the height, picket style, and finish as closely as possible. That attention to detail helps your project meet community expectations and keeps Westwood's streets looking consistent.

## Built to Handle Magnolia Conditions

Clay soil, heavy rain, and summer heat test every fence in Montgomery County. We set posts securely, use quality hardware, and build gates to stay square over time, so your fence keeps looking good long after installation.

## Fence Repair Services

If your fence is leaning, missing pickets, or has a gate that won't close, we can help. We repair what's worth saving and replace what isn't, always in a style that keeps you within your community's guidelines.
