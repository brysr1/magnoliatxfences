Cimarron Country homeowners know that a good fence does a lot of work: it keeps kids and pets safe, adds privacy, and contributes to the overall look of the neighborhood. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs and repairs fences in Cimarron Country and across the Magnolia area, designed to meet community guidelines and built to last.

## HOA-Compliant Fence Design

Like many neighborhoods in Montgomery County, Cimarron Country has community standards that guide what fences can look like. Height limits, approved materials, stain colors, and placement on the lot are common considerations. We help you understand your options, recommend a style that fits those standards, and give you the details you need to submit for approval with confidence.

Fence options for homeowners in Cimarron Country include:

- **Wood privacy fences** in cedar or treated pine, built side-by-side or board-on-board
- **Shadowbox fences** that offer privacy from both sides with a little airflow
- **Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing** for pools and areas where an open view is preferred
- **Single and double gates** for walkways, side yards, and trailer access

## Keeping a Consistent Neighborhood Look

When a fence ties into an existing fence line, small differences in height or finish stand out. We match the style, height, and color of surrounding fences wherever possible so your new fence fits right in with the rest of Cimarron Country.

## Strong Posts, Straight Lines

Magnolia's clay soil moves with wet and dry seasons, and posts that aren't set well will eventually lean. We set posts carefully, use sturdy rails and quality fasteners, and build gates with bracing that keeps them square. It's the kind of construction that keeps a fence looking new for years.

## Repairs for Existing Fences

Storm damage, rotting pickets, leaning sections, and sagging gates are all repairs we handle regularly. For homeowners in Cimarron Country, we'll fix what can be fixed and recommend replacement only when it truly makes more sense, always in a style that stays within community guidelines.
