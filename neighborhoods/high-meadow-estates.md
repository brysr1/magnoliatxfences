High Meadow Estates is acreage country, and fencing a multi-acre lot is a different job than fencing a suburban backyard. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing for acreage properties in High Meadow Estates, planned around how you use your land and built to hold up across long runs and open ground.

## Perimeter Fencing for Larger Lots

On a property measured in acres, the fence line is long and the terrain is rarely perfectly flat. We walk the property with you, confirm where your lines and access points need to be, and recommend materials that balance cost, durability, and appearance across the full run. Corner and brace assemblies are built to keep wire tight and posts straight for years.

## Ranch, Field, and Livestock Fencing

Many homeowners in High Meadow Estates keep horses, cattle, goats, or other animals, or want the option to. We install:

- **No-climb horse fence** that keeps hooves out of the wire and protects horses from injury
- **Field fence and woven wire** for goats, sheep, dogs, and general livestock
- **Barbed wire and pipe fencing** for pastures and cross-fencing
- **Wood or pipe rail fencing** along driveways and front pastures for a classic ranch look

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Acreage doesn't always mean you want everything in view. We build tall wood privacy fences around backyards, pools, and outdoor living areas, and along property lines that border a road or neighbor. On larger properties, we often combine a privacy section near the home with open ranch fencing on the rest of the land.

## Gates for Trucks, Trailers, and Equipment

Wide drive gates, pipe gates, and walk-throughs are part of almost every acreage project. We size gates for tractors, trailers, and livestock trailers, set heavy posts to carry the weight, and can prepare openings for automatic gate operators.

## Repairs After Storms and Wear

Fallen trees, washouts, and curious livestock all take a toll on fence lines. We repair broken wire, replace damaged posts and braces, and restore sections so your property is secure again.
