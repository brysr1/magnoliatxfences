Saddle Creek Forest is the kind of Magnolia-area acreage community where a fence has to do more than mark a property line. It may need to hold horses, keep dogs in and wildlife out, or create a private backyard on a wooded lot. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing for acreage properties in Saddle Creek Forest, designed around your land and how you use it.

## Perimeter Fencing on Wooded and Open Acreage

Fencing a multi-acre lot often means working through trees, around drainage, and across uneven ground. We lay out the fence line with you, plan clearing where needed, and build strong corner and brace assemblies so wire stays tight and posts stay straight across long runs.

## Horse, Livestock, and Field Fencing

For property owners in Saddle Creek Forest with animals, we install:

- **No-climb horse fence** with small openings that keep hooves and legs safe
- **Woven wire field fence** for goats, sheep, and general livestock
- **Pipe fencing and rail fencing** for arenas, round pens, and front pastures
- **Barbed wire and cross-fencing** for cattle and pasture rotation

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Even on acreage, most families want a private outdoor space close to the house. We build cedar and pine privacy fences around backyards, pools, and patios, and along road frontage or neighboring boundaries where screening helps. A privacy fence near the home combined with open ranch fencing elsewhere is a practical, attractive setup.

## Drive Gates and Pasture Gates

We install gates sized for trucks, horse trailers, tractors, and mowers, hung on heavy posts with bracing that prevents sagging. Pipe gates, walk gates, and openings prepared for automatic operators are all available.

## Repairs After Storms and Wear

Wooded lots mean falling limbs, and livestock put steady pressure on fences. We repair or replace broken posts, re-stretch or replace wire, rebuild braces, and fix damaged privacy sections so your fence line is secure again.
