Grand Harbor is a lake community of about 701 homes in the Montgomery and Lake Conroe area, and it's part of the broader service area that 76 Fence of Magnolia covers beyond Magnolia and Pinehurst. Homeowners in Grand Harbor expect a fence to look polished, follow community guidelines, and leave the lake views that drew them here wide open. That's exactly how we approach every project.

## Lakefront-Friendly, HOA-Compliant Fence Styles

In a lake community like Grand Harbor, fence rules typically address height, material, color, and how much of the view a fence can block, especially on waterfront lots. Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing is a common choice because it secures the yard while keeping the lake in view. We help you choose a style that fits your community's standards and give you the details you need for a smooth approval.

## Waterfront and Dock Fencing

For lakefront homes in Grand Harbor, we build fencing that works with the shoreline:

- **Open-style waterfront perimeter fencing** that preserves lake sightlines
- **Gates and fencing near docks and boat lifts** to manage access to the water
- **Fencing that transitions between yard and shoreline** cleanly and safely

## Pool Enclosure Fencing

Pools are a big part of lake living. We install iron and aluminum pool fences with self-closing, self-latching gates, sized and styled to look like a natural extension of your outdoor living space rather than an afterthought.

## Privacy Fencing With a Gated-Community Look

Where privacy fencing is permitted, such as along side yards or between neighbors, we build cedar fences with clean caps, trim, and stain finishes that match the upscale look of the community. Combining privacy near patios with open ornamental fencing toward the lake gives you the best of both.

## Repairs and Refinishing

Lake humidity and wind are tough on fences. We repair rusting iron, loose panels, worn wood, and sagging gates, and we can refinish or replace sections so everything looks consistent again.

<h2>About Grand Harbor</h2>
<p>Grand Harbor is a gated community on a Lake Conroe inlet, with its main gate on Walden Rd and a residents' gate on FM 1097. Custom homes sit on wooded lots of a half acre or more, both waterfront and inland. It's governed by the Grand Harbor Property Owners Association, is in Montgomery ISD, and has a private boat launch and a 10-acre park.</p>
<p>On waterfront lots, open aluminum or steel keeps the lake in view. Inland wooded lots often get cedar privacy along the property lines.</p>

<p>Many Grand Harbor homes sit on or near the water, where open fencing keeps lake views intact. Lake and golf communities like Grand Harbor typically have an HOA or architectural review committee with stricter rules for lots on the water or along a fairway, since those views are shared by the whole community. Approval usually comes before any work, and pool barriers need self-closing, self-latching gates. <div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Permits and jurisdiction</h3><p>We confirm whether your address is inside city limits (the City of Montgomery, for example, requires a fence permit) or in unincorporated county, where a typical residential fence usually doesn't need a county building permit.</p><p>Architectural approval is separate, and lakefront work below the 201-foot contour needs an SJRA permit.</p><p>We request Texas 811 locates before digging.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Site considerations</h3><p>On lakefront lots, the San Jacinto River Authority's Lake Conroe rules treat land up to the 201-foot (MSL) contour as Authority Land, and any permanent structure placed there needs an SJRA permit. Before setting posts near the water, we confirm where your property line and that contour fall.</p><p>Canal and open-water lots both benefit from open fencing that keeps the water in view.</p></div></div>
