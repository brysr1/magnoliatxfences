Lake Windcrest is one of the Magnolia-area communities where homeowners enjoy larger lots and a more rural pace. Fencing that kind of property calls for a different approach than a standard backyard job. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing for acreage properties in Lake Windcrest, built for long runs, varied terrain, and the way you actually use your land.

## Perimeter Fencing Done Right

A perimeter fence on acreage is only as good as its layout and its corners. We walk your Lake Windcrest property with you to confirm boundaries, gate locations, and problem areas like low spots, slopes, and tree lines. Then we build strong brace assemblies and set line posts so the fence stays tight and straight for years.

## Ranch and Livestock Fencing

For homeowners in Lake Windcrest with horses, goats, dogs, or other animals, we offer:

- **No-climb horse fence** for safe, secure horse pastures
- **Field fence and woven wire** for goats, sheep, and mixed livestock
- **Barbed wire** for cattle and long pasture runs
- **Pipe and rail fencing** for pens, arenas, and an attractive front entry

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Wide-open land is great, but most families still want a private backyard. We build tall wood privacy fences around patios, pools, and play areas, and along roadside or neighboring property lines where you want screening. Combining privacy near the home with open ranch fencing across the rest of the property keeps costs reasonable while giving you the best of both.

## Gates for Equipment and Access

Acreage living means trucks, trailers, mowers, and tractors coming and going. We install wide drive gates, pipe gates, and walk gates on heavy, well-braced posts, and can prepare gate openings for automatic operators.

## Fence Repair in Lake Windcrest

High winds, falling limbs, and livestock all wear on fences. We repair broken posts and braces, re-stretch or replace wire, and rebuild damaged privacy sections so your fence is back in shape quickly.
