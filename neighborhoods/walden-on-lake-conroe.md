Walden on Lake Conroe is a large lake and golf community in Montgomery, TX, with a mix of housing styles and new homes still being built. It's part of the Montgomery and Lake Conroe service area that 76 Fence of Magnolia covers, and we help homeowners in Walden on Lake Conroe plan fences that fit community guidelines, suit their style of home, and keep lake and fairway views open.

## Fencing for a Mix of Home Styles

Because Walden on Lake Conroe includes a wide range of homes, from established properties to new construction, fence needs vary from lot to lot. A newer home may need its first full backyard fence, while an older property may need a replacement that matches current community standards. Either way, we review your lot, confirm what your community's guidelines allow, and provide the drawings and material details you need for approval.

## Preserving Lake and Golf Course Sightlines

Lots along the water or the course often require open-style fencing. Ornamental iron and aluminum fences define your yard and add security while letting you, and your neighbors, keep the view. We match height and style to community requirements so your fence looks like it belongs.

## Waterfront, Dock, and Pool Fencing

For lakefront and pool homes in Walden on Lake Conroe, we install:

- **Waterfront perimeter fencing** that follows your lot line without blocking the lake
- **Dock-area fencing and gates** to control access to the water
- **Pool enclosure fencing** with self-closing, self-latching gates
- **Decorative walk and drive gates** that complement the home

## Privacy Fencing With an Upscale Finish

Where privacy fencing is allowed, we build cedar fences with caps, trim, and stain finishes that suit a gated-community aesthetic. Pairing private patio areas with open ornamental fencing toward the lake or fairway is a popular approach.

## New Construction and Repairs

Building a new home in Walden on Lake Conroe? We can coordinate your fence with your move-in timeline. For existing homes, we repair leaning sections, worn pickets, rusting iron, and sagging gates.

<h2>Before you fence in Walden on Lake Conroe</h2>
<div class="cards76">
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Homes and lots</h3><p>Walden is governed by the Walden on Lake Conroe Community Improvement Association across 24 sections, from single-family and estate sections to condominium regimes such as The 18th, The Cove, First Fairway, Harbour Village, Marina Vista, The Point, and The Lodge.</p><p>Section 4's original restrictions date to 1972, and the community is served by Montgomery County MUDs 8 and 9.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>HOA and fence rules</h3><p>Walden's Architectural Control Committee meets every other Tuesday, with requests due by noon the Friday before, submitted through the association's online architectural change request form.</p><p>Proposed updated Section 4 restrictions posted by the association would allow fences only behind the front building line, generally up to 6 feet, with no chain link or wire. On waterfront lots, fences behind the rear of the house would be limited to 4-foot, black, open ornamental metal. Check whether these have been adopted for your section.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Permits and jurisdiction</h3><p>We confirm whether your address is inside city limits (the City of Montgomery, for example, requires a fence permit) or in unincorporated county, where a typical residential fence usually doesn't need a county building permit.</p><p>Architectural approval is separate, and lakefront work below the 201-foot contour needs an SJRA permit.</p><p>We request Texas 811 locates before digging.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Site considerations</h3><p>On lakefront lots, the San Jacinto River Authority's Lake Conroe rules treat land up to the 201-foot (MSL) contour as Authority Land, and any permanent structure placed there needs an SJRA permit. Before setting posts near the water, we confirm where your property line and that contour fall.</p><p>Condo and townhome regimes have their own rules, separate from single-family sections.</p></div></div>
</div>
<p class="note76">Rules are summarized from published HOA documents and recorded deed restrictions and can change. We confirm the current requirements for your lot before we quote.</p>
