Walden on Lake Conroe is a large lake and golf community in Montgomery, TX, with a mix of housing styles and new homes still being built. It's part of the Montgomery and Lake Conroe service area that 76 Fence of Magnolia covers, and we help homeowners in Walden on Lake Conroe plan fences that fit community guidelines, suit their style of home, and keep lake and fairway views open.

## Fencing for a Mix of Home Styles

Because Walden on Lake Conroe includes a wide range of homes, from established properties to new construction, fence needs vary from lot to lot. A newer home may need its first full backyard fence, while an older property may need a replacement that matches current community standards. Either way, we review your lot, confirm what your community's guidelines allow, and provide the drawings and material details you need for approval.

## Preserving Lake and Golf Course Sightlines

Lots along the water or the course often require open-style fencing. Ornamental iron and aluminum fences define your yard and add security while letting you, and your neighbors, keep the view. We match height and style to community requirements so your fence looks like it belongs.

## Waterfront, Dock, and Pool Fencing

For lakefront and pool homes in Walden on Lake Conroe, we install:

- **Waterfront perimeter fencing** that follows your lot line without blocking the lake
- **Dock-area fencing and gates** to control access to the water
- **Pool enclosure fencing** with self-closing, self-latching gates
- **Decorative walk and drive gates** that complement the home

## Privacy Fencing With an Upscale Finish

Where privacy fencing is allowed, we build cedar fences with caps, trim, and stain finishes that suit a gated-community aesthetic. Pairing private patio areas with open ornamental fencing toward the lake or fairway is a popular approach.

## New Construction and Repairs

Building a new home in Walden on Lake Conroe? We can coordinate your fence with your move-in timeline. For existing homes, we repair leaning sections, worn pickets, rusting iron, and sagging gates.
