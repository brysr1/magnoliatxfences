## Fencing for a Mix of Home Styles

Because Walden on Lake Conroe includes a wide range of homes, from established properties to new construction, fence needs vary from lot to lot. A newer home may need its first full backyard fence, while an older property may need a replacement that matches current community standards. Either way, we review your lot, confirm what your community's guidelines allow, and provide the drawings and material details you need for approval.

## Preserving Lake and Golf Course Sightlines

Lots along the water or the course often require open-style fencing. Ornamental iron and aluminum fences define your yard and add security while letting you, and your neighbors, keep the view. We match height and style to community requirements so your fence looks like it belongs.
