Thousand Oaks offers the kind of room to breathe that draws people to the Magnolia area: larger lots, mature trees, and space for animals, shops, and outdoor projects. 76 Fence of Magnolia helps acreage owners in Thousand Oaks fence that land the right way, with perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing designed for multi-acre properties.

## Fencing the Full Property Line

A perimeter fence on acreage is a big investment, so it needs to be laid out carefully and built to last. We start by walking your property in Thousand Oaks with you, marking corners, gates, and any areas where trees, slopes, or drainage need special attention. Then we recommend the right combination of posts, bracing, and fence material for each stretch of the line.

## Ranch and Livestock Fencing

Whether you keep a couple of horses or are planning a small hobby farm, we can build the fence your animals need:

- **No-climb horse fence** for safe, sturdy pasture enclosures
- **Field fence** for goats, sheep, and mixed livestock
- **Barbed wire** for cattle pastures and cross-fencing
- **Pipe fencing** for pens, arenas, and front pastures with a clean, durable finish

## Privacy Where You Want It

Acreage owners in Thousand Oaks often want privacy close to the house while keeping the rest of the property open. We build tall wood privacy fences around backyards, pools, and patios, and along roadside or shared boundaries where screening makes sense. Cedar, pine, and board-on-board styles are all available, with stain options for a finished look.

## Gates Built for Acreage

From wide double drive gates to pipe gates between pastures, we size every gate for the trucks, trailers, and equipment that need to pass through. Heavy-duty hinge posts and proper bracing keep gates from sagging, and we can prepare openings for automatic operators.

## Repair and Maintenance

Wind, falling limbs, and livestock pressure wear on any fence. We repair broken wire, replace rotted or snapped posts, rebuild failing braces, and straighten leaning sections so your fence line stays tight and secure.
