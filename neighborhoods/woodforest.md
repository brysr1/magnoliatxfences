Woodforest is a 5,500-home master-planned community in Montgomery, TX, including the Bonterra 55+ section. It's part of the Montgomery and Lake Conroe service area that 76 Fence of Magnolia covers in addition to Magnolia and Pinehurst. We install and repair HOA-compliant fences for homeowners in Woodforest, built to match the community's look and approved the first time.

## Fences That Meet Woodforest's Design Guidelines

Master-planned communities like Woodforest have design guidelines that keep neighborhoods looking cohesive. Those standards usually cover fence height, materials, stain color, picket style, and placement relative to the home and street. We talk through your goals, recommend a fence that fits within those rules, and provide clear layout and material details for your approval submission. Once approved, we build exactly what was submitted.

Popular fence projects for homes in Woodforest include:

- **Cedar privacy fences** in board-on-board or side-by-side styles, stained to an approved color
- **Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing** for pool areas, golf-view lots, and open side yards
- **Fence replacements** for aging builder fences that have started to lean or rot
- **Walk and double gates** sized for mowers, bins, and small trailers

## Matching the Existing Streetscape

Across a community the size of Woodforest, consistency is what keeps each section looking sharp. When we replace one side of a fence or tie into a neighbor's, we match height, cap style, and finish so the fence line looks continuous.

## Low-Maintenance Options for Bonterra Homeowners

Homeowners in the Bonterra 55+ section of Woodforest often want a fence that looks great with as little upkeep as possible. Aluminum fencing, pre-stained wood, and well-built gates that latch easily are all good fits. We'll recommend options that keep maintenance simple while staying within community guidelines.

## Fence Repair in Woodforest

We fix leaning posts, broken pickets, storm damage, and gates that drag or won't latch. If a section is past saving, we'll replace it in the same approved style.
