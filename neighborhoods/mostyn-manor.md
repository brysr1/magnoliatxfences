Mostyn Manor is a Magnolia neighborhood where homeowners take pride in a well-kept, consistent streetscape. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs and repairs fences for homeowners in Mostyn Manor with that in mind: fences that meet community guidelines, match the surrounding homes, and hold up to years of Texas heat, wind, and rain.

## HOA-Compliant Fence Installation

Before a new fence goes up in a deed-restricted neighborhood like Mostyn Manor, it usually needs approval. Guidelines commonly address fence height, material, picket orientation, stain color, and how far the fence sits back from the front of the home. We walk your property with you, talk through what your community allows, and give you a clear description of the fence style, materials, and layout to submit with your request. Once it's approved, we build it to match.

Fence styles that work well for homes in Mostyn Manor include:

- **Cedar privacy fences** with a clean top cap and trim board for a finished, upscale look
- **Board-on-board fences** that provide full privacy with no gaps as the wood seasons
- **Iron and aluminum fencing** for front yards, pool areas, and side yards where you want security without blocking the view
- **Custom gates** for walkways, driveways, and side-yard equipment access

## Matching Existing Fences and Community Aesthetics

Many Mostyn Manor projects involve replacing one side of a fence, extending a fence line, or tying into a neighbor's fence. We pay attention to height, post spacing, and finish so the new fence looks like it has always been there. If your community specifies a stain color, we can finish the fence to match.

## Fence Repair in Mostyn Manor

Storms, sprinklers, and shifting soil take a toll over time. We fix leaning posts, replace broken or rotted pickets, and rehang gates that drag or won't latch. When a repair is no longer worth it, we'll tell you plainly and quote a replacement that keeps your fence within community standards.

## Local Service, Careful Work

We're based in the Magnolia area and work in neighborhoods like Mostyn Manor every week. You can expect clear quotes, on-time crews, and a clean job site when we're done.
