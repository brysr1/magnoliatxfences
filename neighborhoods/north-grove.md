## Fencing That Fits North Grove's Guidelines

Planned communities like North Grove usually have design guidelines that cover fence height, materials, stain color, and setbacks. Those rules keep the neighborhood looking consistent, but they can make a simple fence project feel complicated. We take care of the details up front: we confirm the approved style with you, prepare a clear layout and material description you can include with your HOA request, and build exactly what was approved.

Common fence projects for homes in North Grove include:

- **Wood privacy fences** in cedar or pine, built board-on-board or side-by-side with a cap and trim option
- **Stained fences** finished in a color that matches your community's approved palette
- **Ornamental metal fencing** for pool areas and lots where an open view makes more sense than a solid wall
- **Walk gates and double gates** for side-yard access, trailers, and lawn equipment

## A Consistent Look Up and Down the Street

When a new fence ties into a neighbor's fence or an existing builder fence line, it should look like one continuous run. We match post spacing, picket height, and finish wherever we can, so the new work blends with the rest of the neighborhood instead of standing out.

## Built for Magnolia Soil and Weather

Heavy clay soil, long wet spells, and summer heat are hard on fences. We set posts deep and solid, use quality fasteners, and build gates with proper bracing so they keep swinging true. If your current fence is already leaning or rotting at the base, we can repair or replace the damaged sections while keeping the approved style.
