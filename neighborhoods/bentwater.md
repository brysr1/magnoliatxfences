Bentwater sits on the west shore of Lake Conroe in Montgomery, TX, and with roughly 2,955 homes and 54 holes of golf, it's one of the best-known lake and golf communities in the area. Bentwater is part of the broader Montgomery and Lake Conroe service area that 76 Fence of Magnolia covers, and we build fences here that respect the community's upscale look and the views that make lake and fairway living special.

## Fences That Preserve Lake and Fairway Views

In a community like Bentwater, a fence should never feel like a wall between you and the water or the golf course. Lakefront and golf-course-frontage lots typically have stricter guidelines on height, material, and openness. Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing is often the best fit: it defines your yard and provides security while keeping sightlines open to the lake or fairway. We help you choose a style and height that fits your community's standards and prepare clear details for your approval request.

## Waterfront, Dock, and Pool Fencing

Lakefront properties in Bentwater have needs that inland lots don't. We install:

- **Waterfront perimeter fencing** that follows the shoreline side of your lot without blocking the view
- **Dock and bulkhead-area fencing and gates** to control access to the water
- **Pool enclosure fencing** in iron or aluminum, built with self-closing, self-latching gates
- **Decorative entry and side-yard gates** that match the home's architecture

## Privacy Fencing With a Gated-Community Aesthetic

Where privacy is allowed and wanted, such as between neighbors or along a side yard, we build cedar privacy fences with clean caps, trim, and stain finishes that suit Bentwater's upscale homes. Mixing privacy panels near patios with open ornamental fencing toward the lake or course is a common, attractive solution.

## Fence Repair

Humidity, lake winds, and sprinkler overspray wear on any fence. We repair loose iron panels, rusting posts, sagging gates, and weathered wood sections so your fence looks right again.
