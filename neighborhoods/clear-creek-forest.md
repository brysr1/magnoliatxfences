Clear Creek Forest is known for wooded acreage lots, and fencing property like this takes planning. Long fence lines run through trees, around drainage, and across uneven ground. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs perimeter, livestock, and privacy fencing for acreage properties in Clear Creek Forest, built to work with the land rather than against it.

## Perimeter Fencing Through Wooded Acreage

Fencing a wooded lot means clearing a clean fence line, working around root systems, and planning for low spots where water collects after heavy rain. We lay out your fence with these conditions in mind, set posts and braces where they'll stay solid, and recommend materials that will last in a shaded, damp environment. The result is a perimeter fence that defines your property and keeps it secure.

## No-Climb, Field, and Livestock Fencing

For homeowners in Clear Creek Forest with horses, goats, chickens, or dogs that need room to roam, we build:

- **No-climb horse fence** with tight mesh that keeps hooves and noses safe
- **Woven wire field fence** for goats, sheep, and general-purpose pasture
- **Welded wire and hog panel enclosures** for gardens, coops, and dog runs
- **Pipe and rail fencing** for arenas, front pastures, and driveway entries

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Even on a large wooded property, most families want a private space near the house. We build tall cedar and pine privacy fences around backyards, pools, and patios, and along road frontage or shared property lines where you want a solid visual barrier. Combining a privacy fence near the home with open ranch fencing across the rest of the acreage is a popular, cost-effective approach.

## Drive Gates and Access Points

Acreage properties in Clear Creek Forest need gates that handle trucks, trailers, and equipment. We install wide drive gates, pipe gates, and walk gates on heavy posts, and can plan openings for automatic gate openers.

## Storm and Tree Damage Repair

On wooded lots, falling limbs are a fact of life. We repair crushed sections, replace snapped posts, and re-tension wire so your fence is back to full strength quickly.
