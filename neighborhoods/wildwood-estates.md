Wildwood Estates offers the room and privacy that draw so many families to acreage living around Magnolia. Making the most of that land usually starts with a good fence. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing for acreage properties in Wildwood Estates, planned around your property's layout and built to last.

## Perimeter Fencing for Acreage

Long fence lines need careful planning. We walk your Wildwood Estates property with you to identify corners, gate locations, tree lines, and drainage areas. From there, we recommend the right materials and construction for each stretch, with solid corner and brace assemblies that keep the fence tight and straight over time.

## Ranch and Livestock Fencing

If you keep animals or plan to, we build the fencing they need:

- **No-climb horse fence** to keep horses safe and secure
- **Woven wire field fence** for goats, sheep, and dogs
- **Barbed wire** for cattle pasture and cross-fencing
- **Pipe fencing** for pens, arenas, and a clean, durable look along the road

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Many acreage owners in Wildwood Estates want privacy around the house, pool, or patio while keeping the rest of the property open. We build tall cedar and pine privacy fences in side-by-side and board-on-board styles, with stain options for a finished appearance. Privacy fencing along a road or neighboring boundary is another common request.

## Gates Built for Acreage Life

From double drive gates wide enough for trailers to pasture gates and walk gates, we size and hang every gate for real-world use. Heavy posts and proper bracing keep gates from sagging, and we can prepare openings for automatic gate operators.

## Fence Repairs

Storms, falling limbs, and animal pressure take a toll on even the best fences. We repair damaged posts and braces, re-stretch or replace wire, and rebuild privacy sections so your property stays secure.
