Acreage properties in Indigo Lake Estates offer space that suburban lots can't, and fencing that space well takes experience with long fence lines, open ground, and the needs of animals and families alike. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fences for property owners in Indigo Lake Estates and across Magnolia's acreage communities.

## Laying Out a Perimeter Fence

Every acreage project starts on foot. We walk your Indigo Lake Estates property with you to mark corners, gates, and any areas where trees, slopes, or drainage need extra attention. From there, we recommend fence materials and post and brace construction that suit each part of the line, balancing durability, appearance, and budget.

## Livestock and Ranch Fencing

Whether your property is home to horses, goats, cattle, or a few backyard chickens, we can build the right enclosure:

- **No-climb horse fence** that protects horses from injury and keeps them contained
- **Field fence** for goats, sheep, and general livestock
- **Barbed wire** for cattle pasture and cross-fencing
- **Welded wire and panel enclosures** for gardens, coops, and dog runs

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

On acreage, privacy fencing is usually about the spaces closest to home. We build tall wood privacy fences around backyards, pools, patios, and outdoor kitchens, and along roads or neighboring property where a solid screen makes sense. Many homeowners in Indigo Lake Estates pair a privacy fence near the house with open ranch fencing across the rest of their land.

## Gates Sized for Real Use

We install wide double drive gates, pipe gates between pastures, and walk gates for everyday access. Gates are hung on heavy posts with proper bracing to prevent sagging, and openings can be prepared for automatic operators.

## Storm Damage and Fence Repair

Falling branches, high winds, and pressure from animals can take a fence line down fast. We repair or replace damaged posts, braces, wire, and boards so your property is secure again.
