As the name suggests, Ranch Crest is a place where land matters. Property owners here often need fencing that can handle animals, define long property lines, and still create a comfortable private space around the home. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing for acreage properties in Ranch Crest and throughout the Magnolia area.

## Perimeter Fencing Built for the Long Run

A good perimeter fence on acreage starts with a solid plan. We walk your Ranch Crest property with you to set corners, mark gate openings, and spot any trees, slopes, or wet areas along the line. Strong corner and H-brace assemblies keep wire tight, and properly spaced line posts keep the fence straight from one end to the other.

## Livestock and Field Fencing

We build fencing for horses, cattle, goats, sheep, and working dogs, including:

- **No-climb horse fence** to protect horses and keep them contained
- **Woven wire field fence** for small livestock and general use
- **Barbed wire** for cattle pasture and cross-fencing
- **Pipe and rail fencing** for arenas, pens, and a classic ranch entrance

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Not every part of an acreage property needs to be open. Many homeowners in Ranch Crest want a private backyard, pool area, or patio screened from the road or neighbors. We build tall cedar and pine privacy fences in several styles, with stain options, and pair them with ranch fencing on the rest of the property.

## Gates for Trucks, Trailers, and Tractors

We install wide drive gates, pipe pasture gates, and walk gates hung on heavy, well-braced posts so they swing true and don't sag. Gate openings can also be prepared for automatic operators.

## Fence Repair and Maintenance

Weather, falling limbs, and livestock all take a toll over time. We repair broken posts and braces, re-stretch or replace wire, and fix damaged privacy fencing to keep your property secure.
