Ranch Crest is a Magnolia subdivision off FM 1774, and despite the name, most homes here sit on typical neighborhood lots rather than acreage. That means the fences that matter most are backyard privacy fences, side-yard gates, and replacements for fences that went up when the homes were built. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds and repairs fences in Ranch Crest that fit the neighborhood's guidelines and hold up to our soil and weather.

## Backyard Privacy Fences in Ranch Crest

A six-foot cedar privacy fence is the standard backyard fence in neighborhoods like Ranch Crest. We build it with three rails, a rot board along the bottom, and posts set deep in concrete, so it stays straight and keeps its pickets tight. If your HOA specifies a picket style, cap, or stain color, we build to match and help you put together what the board needs to approve it.

Common projects in Ranch Crest include:

- **Cedar privacy fences** in side-by-side or board-on-board styles
- **Replacement of original builder fences** that are leaning, graying, or losing pickets
- **Side-yard and drive gates** wide enough for mowers, trash bins, and trailers
- **Pet fencing** with dig barriers and self-latching gates

## Replacing an Original Fence

Many Ranch Crest homes are now ten to twenty years old, and so are their fences. When a fence has leaning posts or rot along much of the line, patching only buys a little time. We'll look at the whole fence and tell you plainly whether a repair will hold or a replacement makes more sense, and we haul the old fence away as part of any replacement.

## Shared Fence Lines

On neighborhood lots, most fence lines are shared with a neighbor. We can work with both households on a shared line, match the existing fence where a new section ties in, and keep the work tidy on both sides of the fence.

<h2>Before you fence in Ranch Crest</h2>
<div class="cards76">
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Homes and lots</h3><p>Ranch Crest's newer sections (III and IV) were platted in 2013 and 2015, with many single-story homes built from 2014 to 2016 on streets like Atascosa, Encinal, Wichita, and Del Rio Trails. The older Section 1 area around Pecos Ranch dates to about 2000.</p><p>The Ranch Crest Homeowners' Association Sections III-IV manages the newer sections; the older Section 1 area around Pecos Ranch isn't part of that association.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>HOA and fence rules</h3><p>The Section III and IV deed restrictions require ACC approval before building a fence. Fences can't extend past the front of the house, and street-facing fences must line up uniformly.</p><p>Maximum height is 8 feet (5 feet for an approved variance fence past the front line). Wood, brick, wrought iron, and vinyl are allowed; chain link, wire, barbed wire, and stand-alone mesh are not. Wood fences may be sealed but not painted, and dog runs must be behind the house.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Permits and jurisdiction</h3><p>If your address is inside City of Magnolia limits, the city requires a fence permit ($65 plus a $50 administrative fee), and the application asks for the fence's length, material, and height.</p><p>Outside city limits, in unincorporated Montgomery County, a typical residential fence usually doesn't need a county building permit.</p><p>If the fence line crosses a mapped 100-year floodplain, the county can require a floodplain development permit; three-strand barbed wire fences are exempt.</p><p>HOA or POA approval is separate from any permit, and we request Texas 811 locates before digging.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Site considerations</h3><p>Builder-installed property-line fences are maintained by the adjoining owners, so a shared-line replacement is usually a conversation between neighbors.</p><p>Plat-shown drainage and utility easements and roadside ditches must stay clear; culverts must be at least 18 inches.</p></div></div>
</div>
<p class="note76">Rules are summarized from published HOA documents and recorded deed restrictions and can change. We confirm the current requirements for your lot before we quote.</p>
