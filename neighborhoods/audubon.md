Audubon is one of Magnolia's newer master-planned neighborhoods, and like most planned communities, it comes with design guidelines that shape what a fence can look like. 76 Fence of Magnolia builds fences for homeowners in Audubon that look like they belong on the street, meet community standards, and stand up to Southeast Texas weather for years.

## HOA-Compliant Fences in Audubon

Most Audubon homeowners are working with a builder fence or a lot that needs its first full backyard enclosure. Either way, the fence has to fit within the community's design guidelines, which typically cover height, material, stain color, picket style, and where the fence can sit relative to the front of the house. We help you sort out those details before any posts go in the ground, so your HOA submission is clear and complete.

Popular options for homes in Audubon include:

- **Cedar privacy fences** in board-on-board or side-by-side styles, stained to match the community's approved colors
- **Ornamental iron or aluminum fencing** for side yards, pool areas, and lots where a view should stay open
- **Gates** sized for mowers, trash bins, and side-yard access, built to close cleanly without sagging

## Matching the Look of the Neighborhood

In a community like Audubon, consistency matters. A fence that matches the existing builder fence line in height, cap style, and color keeps the streetscape uniform and helps protect property values for everyone. When we extend, replace, or tie into an existing fence, we match what's already there as closely as possible so the new section doesn't stand out.

## Repairs and Replacements

Builder-grade fences in newer neighborhoods often need attention sooner than homeowners expect. Leaning sections, loose pickets, and posts that shift in our clay soil are common. We repair what can be saved and replace what can't, keeping the same approved style so you stay within your community guidelines.

## Why Audubon Homeowners Call Us

We're local to Magnolia, we understand how planned communities in this part of Montgomery County handle fence approvals, and we build every project to last. From a single gate repair to a full backyard enclosure, homeowners in Audubon get the same careful planning and clean installation.

<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Permits and jurisdiction</h3><p>Audubon sits in the City of Magnolia's extraterritorial jurisdiction (ETJ), next to the city.</p><p>If your address is inside City of Magnolia limits, the city requires a fence permit ($65 plus a $50 administrative fee), and the application asks for the fence's length, material, and height.</p><p>Outside city limits, in unincorporated Montgomery County, a typical residential fence usually doesn't need a county building permit.</p><p>HOA or POA approval is separate from any permit, and we request Texas 811 locates before digging.</p></div></div>
