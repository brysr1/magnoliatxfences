## HOA-Compliant Fences in Audubon

Most Audubon homeowners are working with a builder fence or a lot that needs its first full backyard enclosure. Either way, the fence has to fit within the community's design guidelines, which typically cover height, material, stain color, picket style, and where the fence can sit relative to the front of the house. We help you sort out those details before any posts go in the ground, so your HOA submission is clear and complete.

Popular options for homes in Audubon include:

- **Cedar privacy fences** in board-on-board or side-by-side styles, stained to match the community's approved colors
- **Ornamental iron or aluminum fencing** for side yards, pool areas, and lots where a view should stay open
- **Gates** sized for mowers, trash bins, and side-yard access, built to close cleanly without sagging

## Matching the Look of the Neighborhood

In a community like Audubon, consistency matters. When we extend, replace, or tie into an existing fence, we match what's already there as closely as possible so the new section doesn't stand out.

We repair what can be saved and replace what can't, keeping the same approved style so you stay within your community guidelines.
