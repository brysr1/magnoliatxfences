## Fence Styles That Respect Lake Views

Around Lake Conroe, a fence that blocks the view can feel like a step backward. For lakefront and lake-view lots in Cape Conroe, open-style ornamental iron and aluminum fencing is often the best fit. It marks your property line and adds security while keeping the water in sight. We'll help you choose a height and style that fits your community's standards and provide the details you need if approval is required.

## Waterfront, Dock, and Pool Fencing

Life near the lake brings specific fencing needs. For Cape Conroe homeowners, we install:

- **Waterfront perimeter fencing** designed to keep sightlines open
- **Fencing and gates near docks and bulkheads** to manage access to the water
- **Private pool enclosures** with self-closing, self-latching gates for safety
- **Decorative walk and drive gates** that complement the home

## Privacy Fencing With a Clean, Upscale Look

Where privacy is the goal, such as along side yards, between neighbors, or around a patio, we build cedar privacy fences with caps, trim, and stain finishes for a polished appearance. Many lake homeowners combine privacy near outdoor living areas with open ornamental fencing toward the water.

## Built for Humidity and Lake Weather

Lake humidity and wind are tough on fences. We use rust-resistant hardware, set posts securely, and build gates with bracing that keeps them square, so your fence keeps looking good season after season.

## Fence Repairs in Cape Conroe

We repair rusting iron, loose panels, weathered wood, leaning posts, and gates that drag or won't latch, keeping your fence consistent with the rest of your property.
