We install and repair fences for homeowners in Carriage Hills that fit community expectations, look great from the street, and stand up to Southeast Texas weather.

## Fencing Within Community Guidelines

Even in a neighborhood with lighter deed restrictions, it's smart to check what's allowed before a fence goes up. Guidelines may still address fence height, materials, or placement near the street. We'll talk through your goals, help you confirm what your community expects, and recommend a fence style that fits. If approval is needed, we'll provide a clear description of the fence and layout to submit.

Popular fence options for homeowners in Carriage Hills include:

- **Cedar and pine privacy fences** in side-by-side, board-on-board, or shadowbox styles
- **Stained fences** for added protection and a finished look
- **Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing** for pools, front yards, and open-view areas
- **Chain link fencing** for practical pet and utility enclosures where permitted
- **Walk and drive gates** sized for mowers, trailers, and everyday access

## Matching the Neighborhood's Look

A fence that fits the character of the street adds value to your home and the neighborhood. When we tie into an existing fence or replace part of one, we match height, style, and finish so the result looks consistent.

## Durable Construction

Montgomery County's clay soils and heavy rains are hard on fence posts. We set posts securely, use quality fasteners and hardware, and brace gates so they stay square and close cleanly for years.

## Fence Repair in Carriage Hills

From leaning sections and broken pickets to storm damage and sagging gates, we handle repairs of every size. When replacement is the better option, we'll explain why and quote a new fence that suits your home.
