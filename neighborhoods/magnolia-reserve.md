## Quality That Holds Up

Southeast Texas weather is tough on fences. Heavy rain, heat, and shifting clay soil can loosen posts and warp boards on poorly built fences. We set posts solidly, use durable hardware, and build gates with bracing so your fence stays straight and secure.

## Fence Repairs in Magnolia Reserve

We repair leaning posts, broken or rotted pickets, storm damage, and gates that sag or won't latch. If replacement is the smarter choice, we'll explain why and build it in the same approved style.
