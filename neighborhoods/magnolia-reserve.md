Magnolia Reserve is one of the residential communities we serve in the Magnolia area, and homeowners here want fencing that adds privacy and curb appeal while staying within neighborhood guidelines. 76 Fence of Magnolia designs, installs, and repairs HOA-compliant fences for homeowners in Magnolia Reserve, with a focus on doing it right the first time.

## A Fence That Fits Your Community's Rules

Neighborhood guidelines exist to keep a community looking cohesive, and they often cover fence height, material, color, and placement. We'll help you sort through those details before work begins. After a quick visit to understand your property and goals, we recommend a fence style that fits your community's standards and provide the information you need for your approval request.

Fence options we install in Magnolia Reserve include:

- **Cedar and pine privacy fences** in side-by-side, board-on-board, and shadowbox styles
- **Stained finishes** in approved colors to protect the wood and match the neighborhood
- **Ornamental iron and aluminum fencing** for pools, patios, and open-view side yards
- **Gates** built to the right width for your walkways, side yards, and equipment

## Designed to Match the Streetscape

The best fences in a planned neighborhood look like they were part of the original design. When we connect to a neighbor's fence or replace part of an existing fence line, we match height, picket style, and finish so the new section blends in naturally. That consistency helps homeowners in Magnolia Reserve keep their street looking sharp.

## Quality That Holds Up

Southeast Texas weather is tough on fences. Heavy rain, heat, and shifting clay soil can loosen posts and warp boards on poorly built fences. We set posts solidly, use durable hardware, and build gates with bracing so your fence stays straight and secure.

## Fence Repairs in Magnolia Reserve

We repair leaning posts, broken or rotted pickets, storm damage, and gates that sag or won't latch. If replacement is the smarter choice, we'll explain why and build it in the same approved style.
