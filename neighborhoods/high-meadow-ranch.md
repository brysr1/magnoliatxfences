Larger lots and open space are a big part of the appeal of High Meadow Ranch, and the right fence makes that space more useful, more private, and more secure. 76 Fence of Magnolia installs perimeter, ranch, livestock, and privacy fencing for properties in High Meadow Ranch and the surrounding Magnolia acreage communities.

## Perimeter Fencing for Bigger Properties

On a larger lot, the fence line is a major part of the project, and doing it right starts with a careful layout. We walk your property in High Meadow Ranch with you to confirm corners, gate locations, and any trees, slopes, or low spots along the way. Then we recommend the right posts, braces, and fence materials for each section so the whole line stays straight and tight.

## Ranch and Livestock Fencing

If you keep horses or other animals, or want the flexibility to in the future, we build fencing made for the job:

- **No-climb horse fence** designed to keep horses safe and contained
- **Woven wire field fence** for goats, sheep, dogs, and mixed use
- **Pipe and rail fencing** for pens, arenas, and front-of-property curb appeal
- **Cross-fencing** to divide pastures or separate animals from yards and gardens

## Privacy Fencing on Multi-Acre Lots

Many homeowners in High Meadow Ranch want privacy near the house while keeping the rest of the land open. We build tall cedar and pine privacy fences around backyards, pools, and outdoor living spaces, and along road frontage or shared boundaries where screening makes sense. Stain options give the fence a finished look that complements the home.

## Gates for Every Use

Acreage properties need gates that can handle trucks, trailers, mowers, and livestock. We install wide drive gates, pipe gates, and walk gates on heavy posts with proper bracing, and we can prepare openings for automatic gate operators.

## Fence Repair

Storms, falling limbs, and everyday wear can damage any fence. We replace broken posts and braces, repair or re-stretch wire, and fix damaged privacy sections so your property is secure again.
