April Sound is an established lake and golf community on the west shore of Lake Conroe in Montgomery, TX, home to roughly 7,000 residents. It's part of the Montgomery and Lake Conroe service area that 76 Fence of Magnolia covers alongside Magnolia and Pinehurst. We build fences for homeowners in April Sound that fit the community's guidelines, protect lake and fairway views, and match the established character of the neighborhood.

## HOA- and Golf-Frontage-Compliant Fencing

Homes along the water or the golf course in April Sound usually face tighter rules on fence height, material, and transparency, because those views matter to the whole community. Open-style ornamental iron and aluminum fencing is often the right answer: it marks your property line and adds security without closing off the lake or fairway. We'll help you select a style that fits your community's standards and provide the layout and material details you need for approval.

## Waterfront and Pool Enclosures

Lakefront living brings its own fencing needs. For homeowners in April Sound we install:

- **Waterfront perimeter fencing** designed to keep sightlines to the lake open
- **Fencing and gates near docks and boathouses** to manage access to the water
- **Pool enclosure fencing** with self-closing, self-latching gates for safety
- **Ornamental entry gates** that complement the style of the home

## Privacy That Still Feels Upscale

Privacy fencing between neighbors, around patios, or along a side yard can still look refined. We build cedar privacy fences with clean caps and trim, finished in stain colors that suit the community. Many April Sound homeowners combine privacy sections near outdoor living areas with open ornamental fencing toward the water or course.

## Updating and Repairing Older Fences

In an established community, many fences have been in place for years. We replace worn wood sections, refinish or replace rusting iron, reset leaning posts, and rebuild sagging gates, keeping the look consistent with the rest of your property.
