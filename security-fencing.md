<h2>A different job than a backyard fence</h2>
<p>A security fence isn't about privacy or curb appeal first. It's about a continuous perimeter with no easy places to climb, sightlines that work with your lighting and cameras, and gates you can actually control. Shops, storage yards, equipment lots, job sites, churches, and schools around Magnolia and the FM 1488 and SH 249 corridors usually need a different spec than a home.</p>
<p>Every site is different, so we walk the property line with you first. Byron Dean, our General Manager, typically confirms measurements, access points, and any existing security features before we recommend a spec.</p>
</div>
<ul class="checklist76">
<li><strong>Typical height:</strong> 6 to 8 ft, taller for higher-security sites</li>
<li><strong>Tops:</strong> flat, spear-top, angled, or barbed-wire arms</li>
<li><strong>Access:</strong> manual or automatic swing and slide gates, with keypad, card, or call-box entry</li>
</ul>

<h2>Design details</h2>
<ul class="checklist76">
<li>Anti-climb tops matched to the site, from spear-top steel to angled or barbed-wire arms on chain link</li>
<li>Mesh size and wire gauge chosen for what the fence needs to resist</li>
<li>Privacy slats or solid panels where blocking sightlines matters</li>
<li>Black or bronze finishes on steel and aluminum</li>
<li>Swing gates for standard openings and slide gates for wide ones</li>
<li>Concrete footings sized for the height and wind exposure of the run</li>
<li>Coordination with fire department and emergency-access needs on gated sites</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div>
<h2>Gates are the weak point</h2>
<p>An otherwise solid perimeter is only as good as its gates. Where a site calls for it, we pair security fencing with real access control instead of a simple latch: automatic operators, keypads, card readers, or call boxes. See <a href="/fence-styles/gates/">gates &amp; gate openers</a>.</p>
<h2>Homes and acreage, too</h2>
<p>The same ideas work at home: taller fence lines, solid panels, and controlled gates around pools, equipment yards, and rural properties. Pool barriers have their own requirements; see <a href="/fence-styles/pool-enclosure/">pool enclosures</a>.</p>
