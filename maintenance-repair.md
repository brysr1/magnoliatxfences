<h2>Why fences fail out here</h2>
<p>Montgomery County is hard on fences. Clay soil swells when it's wet and shrinks in a dry summer, which slowly pushes posts out of plumb. Long stretches of humidity rot pickets and rails from the bottom up, especially where sprinklers hit the same boards every morning. And a single tropical storm or a falling pine limb can take out a whole section overnight.</p>
<p>When you call us for a repair, we look at the whole fence &mdash; not just the broken part. If the rest of the fence is solid, we'll repair the damaged section to match. If the posts are failing along the entire line, we'll tell you plainly that a patch won't last and show you what a replacement would cost instead.</p>
