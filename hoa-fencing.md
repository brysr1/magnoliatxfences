<h2>Built for communities with rules</h2>
<p>A large share of the homes we work on sit in a deed-restricted neighborhood with an HOA, an architectural review committee (ARC), or both. That includes master-planned communities like <a href="/neighborhoods/audubon/">Audubon</a> and <a href="/neighborhoods/woodforest/">Woodforest</a>, established subdivisions like <a href="/neighborhoods/north-grove/">North Grove</a>, <a href="/neighborhoods/mostyn-manor/">Mostyn Manor</a>, and <a href="/neighborhoods/magnolia-ridge/">Magnolia Ridge</a>, and plenty of smaller neighborhoods that never touch a lake or a fairway.</p>
<p>The guidelines vary, but most cover the same ground: fence height, approved materials, stain color, picket style, whether the good side faces out, and how far the fence has to sit back from the front of the house. We start by reading the rules that apply to your lot, then recommend a fence that meets them. If your committee wants a site plan, product specs, or a color sample, we'll help assemble it so your application isn't sent back for missing details.</p>

<section class="section76">
<h2>Golf-course and lakefront lots</h2>
<p class="section-intro76">In lake and golf communities like <a href="/neighborhoods/bentwater/">Bentwater</a>, <a href="/neighborhoods/april-sound/">April Sound</a>, and <a href="/neighborhoods/walden-on-lake-conroe/">Walden on Lake Conroe</a>, lots on the water or along a fairway often have stricter limits because the view belongs to the whole community. Open-style fencing is usually the answer.</p>
<div class="cards76">
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Sightline-preserving styles</h3><p>Ornamental aluminum and steel with slim pickets and low profiles that keep the lake or fairway in view.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Waterfront perimeter fencing</h3><p>Fencing that follows the shoreline side of the lot, plus gates where the yard meets a bulkhead or dock.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Pool enclosures</h3><p>Pool barriers with self-closing, self-latching gates, built to the local code and HOA requirements that apply to your property.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Neighbor-side privacy</h3><p>Cedar privacy sections between homes, paired with open fencing toward the view.</p></div></div>
