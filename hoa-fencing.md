<h2>Built for communities with rules</h2>
<p>A lot of the neighborhoods we serve have an HOA, an architectural review committee (ARC), or both. Their guidelines usually cover fence height, material, color, picket style, and where a fence can sit on the lot. On the west side of Lake Conroe, in communities like <a href="/neighborhoods/bentwater/">Bentwater</a>, <a href="/neighborhoods/april-sound/">April Sound</a>, and <a href="/neighborhoods/walden-on-lake-conroe/">Walden on Lake Conroe</a>, there's another layer: lots on the water or along a fairway often have stricter limits because the view belongs to the whole community.</p>
<p>We start by reading the guidelines that apply to your lot, then recommend a fence that meets them. If your committee wants a site plan, product specs, or a color sample, we'll help assemble it so your application isn't sent back for missing details.</p>

<h2>Golf-course and lakefront fencing</h2>
<p class="section-intro76">Open-style fencing is usually the answer on the water or the course. It marks the property line and keeps pets and kids in without putting up a wall.</p>
<div class="cards76">
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Sightline-preserving styles</h3><p>Ornamental aluminum and steel with slim pickets and low profiles that keep the lake or fairway in view.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Waterfront perimeter fencing</h3><p>Fencing that follows the shoreline side of the lot, plus gates where the yard meets a bulkhead or dock.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Pool enclosures</h3><p>Pool barriers with self-closing, self-latching gates, built to the local code and HOA requirements that apply to your property.</p></div></div>
