76 Fence of Magnolia builds and repairs fences for homeowners and landowners across Magnolia, Pinehurst, and the surrounding acreage communities of Montgomery County. We also serve the town of Montgomery and the Lake Conroe area, including Bentwater, April Sound, Walden, Grand Harbor, Woodforest, and the nearby lake and golf communities.

## Residential and Acreage Fencing

Whether you need a backyard privacy fence on a quarter-acre lot or several thousand feet of perimeter fencing on a multi-acre property, we plan every project around how you actually use your land. For ranch and acreage owners, we install field fencing, no-climb horse fence, livestock fencing, and long runs of privacy fencing that hold up to Southeast Texas weather.

## Fence Styles We Install

- **Wood privacy fences** — cedar and pine board-on-board, side-by-side, and shadowbox styles with sturdy posts set for our clay soils.
- **Iron and aluminum fencing** — clean, low-maintenance ornamental fencing for front yards, pools, and properties where you want security without blocking the view.
- **Chain link** — a practical, affordable choice for pets, kids, utility areas, and commercial lots.
- **Gates** — walk gates, double drive gates, and automated entry gates, plus repair and re-hanging for gates that sag, drag, or won't latch.

## HOA-Compliant Fencing for Golf and Lakefront Communities

Many of the neighborhoods we serve have design guidelines that set fence height, material, stain color, and placement. Around Lake Conroe and along golf course frontage, those rules often exist to protect water and fairway views. We help you choose a style that fits your community's standards and keeps your approval process simple.

We work in HOA-governed subdivisions, unrestricted acreage properties, and gated lake communities alike — so whatever your property and whatever the rules, we can build a fence that fits.

## Fence Repair

Leaning sections, rotted pickets, storm damage, and broken posts happen. We repair existing fences and gates so you don't have to replace more than you need to.
