<p><strong>Effective date:</strong> October 8, 2026</p>

<p>This policy explains what information 76 Fence of Magnolia (&ldquo;we,&rdquo; &ldquo;us&rdquo;) collects through magnoliatxfences.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.</p>

<h2>Information you give us</h2>
<p>When you send a message through our contact form, book an estimate, call, or text us, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, property address, and details about your project. We use this information to respond to you, schedule and prepare estimates, perform the work you hire us for, and follow up about your project.</p>

<h2>Information collected automatically</h2>
<p>Like most websites, this site and the services it relies on automatically receive some technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and the site that referred you.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Google Analytics.</strong> We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. You can learn how Google uses this data at <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites</a>, and you can opt out with the <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout">Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Hosting.</strong> The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Third-party services on this site</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Contact form:</strong> messages sent through our contact form are delivered to us by Formspree.</li>
<li><strong>Online booking:</strong> the &ldquo;Book a Free Estimate&rdquo; buttons take you to our Jobber client hub, where information you enter is handled by Jobber on our behalf.</li>
<li><strong>Maps:</strong> our service area pages include embedded Google Maps, which may set cookies and collect information under Google's privacy policy.</li>
<li><strong>Images:</strong> some photos are loaded from Unsplash, which may receive basic technical information from your browser when an image loads.</li>
<li><strong>Financing:</strong> if you apply for financing, your application goes to Hearth and is governed by Hearth's own terms and privacy policy.</li>
</ul>
<p>Each of these services has its own privacy policy, which governs the information it collects.</p>

<h2>How we share information</h2>
<p>We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above as needed to run the site and our business, with other members of the 76 Fence family when that's needed to serve your project, when you ask us to, or when required by law.</p>

<h2>Cookies and your choices</h2>
<p>You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work without them, though analytics and embedded maps may not. You can also ask us to update or delete the contact information you've given us by calling or writing to us at the number or address below.</p>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>This site is intended for adults planning fence projects and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.</p>
