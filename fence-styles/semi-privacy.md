<h2>What it looks like</h2>
<p>A semi-privacy fence blocks most of the view without being completely solid. Common versions include shadowbox fences (boards alternating on each side of the rails), solid lower panels topped with lattice, and spaced pickets or slats with small, even gaps.</p>
<p>Most semi-privacy fences are five to six feet tall. They're a good middle ground when a full privacy fence would feel too closed in, when you want breeze through the yard in summer, or when a garden needs light along the fence line.</p>
</div>
<ul class="checklist76">
<li><strong>Privacy level:</strong> Partial to most privacy</li>
<li><strong>Typical height:</strong> 5&ndash;6 ft typical</li>
</ul>
</section>

<h2>Acreage or subdivision lot?</h2>
<div class="cards76">
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>On acreage</h3><p>On acreage, semi-privacy fencing is a good choice around outdoor living areas, gardens, and pool decks, where you want some screening but don't want to block the view of your own land.</p></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>On subdivision lots</h3><p>On subdivision lots, shadowbox and lattice-top fences are often allowed alongside standard privacy styles. If your property has HOA or deed restrictions, check with your board on whether lattice tops or spacing are allowed.</p></div></div>
</div>
