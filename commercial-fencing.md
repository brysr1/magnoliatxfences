<h2>Materials</h2>
<p class="section-intro76">We pick the material by what the fence has to stop and who sees it. Our guide to <a href="/resources/security-fence-types/">security fence types</a> compares them side by side.</p>
<div class="cards76 cards76-3">
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Commercial chain link</h3><p>Heavier fabric than a home fence, commonly 9-gauge, on thicker posts with a bottom tension wire so the fabric can't be lifted. Privacy slats hide inventory, but they catch wind, so slatted runs get stronger, deeper posts.</p><a class="card76-link" href="/materials/chain-link/">Chain link &rarr;</a></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Ornamental steel</h3><p>Powder-coated steel with spear or pressed-point tops and close pickets. It's a real deterrent that still looks finished, and commercial-grade panels are heavier than residential. We keep the smooth face toward the outside so the rails don't give a climber a foothold.</p><a class="card76-link" href="/materials/ornamental-steel-aluminum/">Ornamental steel &rarr;</a></div></div>
<div class="card76"><div class="card76-body"><h3>Welded mesh &amp; aluminum</h3><p>Welded anti-climb mesh for equipment, utilities, and high-value storage where climbing is the real worry. Aluminum is the lighter, rust-free choice around pools and near the water.</p><a class="card76-link" href="/materials/aluminum/">Aluminum &rarr;</a></div></div>
</div>
<p>For <a href="/dumpster-enclosures/">dumpster enclosures</a>, we build in cedar board-on-board, steel panel, or vinyl, sized to the container with double gates wide enough for the hauler's truck. Steel holds up better than wood to repeated bumps from containers and equipment.</p>
<p>Montgomery County's sandy topsoil over clay doesn't hold a post well on its own. On commercial runs we set gate, corner, and enclosure posts deeper and wider, with concrete footings sized for the fence's height and wind exposure.</p>

<h2>Approvals</h2>
<p>Where the property sits decides the rules, and a Magnolia mailing address doesn't mean the lot is inside city limits. The City of Magnolia issues fence permits, and commercial sites inside the city may also have screening requirements tied to their site plan. A lot of FM 1488 and FM 1774 frontage is in unincorporated Montgomery County instead, where the county's floodplain rules can still require a development permit for work in a mapped floodplain. Inside the City of Montgomery, fence is on the city's list of required permits.</p>
<p>Association pools and amenity centers have their own state rules for barriers and gates. We check the jurisdiction for your address before we quote. Our <a href="/faq/fence-permits-magnolia-montgomery/">permit guide</a> explains who governs which lots and links the city and county sources.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h2>Gates and access</h2>
<p>On a commercial site, the gates take more wear than the rest of the fence. We size the vehicle opening for the longest rig that uses it, and set the gate back far enough that a truck isn't stopped in a lane of FM 1488 while it opens. A cantilever slide gate keeps the full opening clear with no ground track for sand and gravel to jam.</p>
<ul class="checklist76">
<li>Swing and slide operators with keypad, remote, or phone-app entry</li>
<li>Solar openers where there's no power at the gate, wired for heavy gates that cycle all day</li>
<li>Safety sensors, a manual release, and UL 325 entrapment protection</li>
<li>A separate walk gate for people on foot</li>
<li>Fire department access planned on gated sites</li>
</ul>
