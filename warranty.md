<p>76 Fence of Magnolia stands behind the way we build. Every fence installation our team completes is covered by an <strong>18-month limited workmanship warranty</strong>, starting on the date the project is completed.</p>

<h2>What's covered</h2>
<p>This warranty covers defects in workmanship only. If a problem results directly from improper installation by our crew within the 18-month period, we'll repair or correct the affected area at no cost to you.</p>

<h2>What's not covered</h2>
<p>The warranty does not cover damage or deterioration caused by, among other things:</p>
<ul>
<li>Acts of God, including hurricanes, floods, tornadoes, lightning, and other natural events</li>
<li>Weather conditions such as high winds, heavy rain, extreme temperatures, or storm damage</li>
<li>Normal aging or natural wear of materials</li>
<li>Customer misuse, neglect, lack of maintenance, or unauthorized repairs or modifications</li>
<li>Soil movement, ground shifting, heaving, or erosion</li>
<li>Impact from outside forces such as vehicles, equipment, or fallen trees and branches</li>
<li>Tree roots or vegetation overgrowth</li>
</ul>

<h2>Manufacturer warranties</h2>
<p>Warranties on materials such as lumber, metal, hardware, and composite products come directly from the manufacturer and are separate from our workmanship warranty. Coverage and terms vary by manufacturer.</p>

<h2>How to file a claim</h2>
<p>Contact us with a short description of the issue and clear photos of the affected area. We'll schedule an inspection to evaluate the claim and confirm whether it's covered.</p>
<p>One thing that isn't a warranty claim at all: gates sometimes stop latching cleanly as new posts settle. That's expected, and we're happy to come out and adjust the gate free of charge.</p>
