<h2>A fence that fits the yard you have</h2>
<p>Most homeowners call us for one of four reasons: they want privacy from the street or the neighbors, they need to keep a dog or kids safely in the yard, they're adding a pool, or the builder fence that came with the house has started to lean. Each of those calls for a slightly different fence, and we'll talk through which one makes sense before recommending anything.</p>
<p>Lots in Magnolia and Pinehurst run the full range &mdash; tight backyards in newer subdivisions, wooded half-acre lots, and homes that back up to drainage easements or greenbelts. We measure the actual fence line, note grade changes and trees, and plan gate locations around mowers, trash bins, and the way you walk the property.</p>
<p>Because many neighborhoods here are deed restricted, we also confirm what your HOA allows for height, style, and stain color, so the fence you choose is one you can actually build.</p>
